Dev Diary

Progress notes on Mark 16 and Narumasouranroku.

2026.11.14

Mark16Manager updated to v0.1.1

We updated the Mark16Manager beta to v0.1.1. Builds are available for Windows and Mac (Apple silicon and Intel).

Thumbnails are now 16-colour, using the Mega Drive’s 512-colour palette (previously four colours), with a preview closer to the console display. Older four-colour thumbnails can still be read.

Japanese intro text now follows the console’s oidashi line-break rules, and combining dakuten is counted as one character, matching the console. The app also notes that cartridge boot is RAM-copy only, and that cartridge saves are stored under /MDTANG/SAVES/.

App page & downloads →

2026.11.09

Mark16Manager PC app beta released

We released the beta (v0.1.0) of Mark16Manager, a companion app for organising a Mark 16 microSD card on your computer. Builds are available for Windows and Mac (Apple silicon and Intel).

Connect the card with a reader to add and reorder games, edit display names, intros and thumbnails, and back up saves. Japanese and English intros are edited separately, with a live preview that follows the same line-break rules as the console menu. Thumbnails are prepared to match how they look on the device.

You can also stage firmware update files on the card and export screenshots already stored there. Console-side SD updates and in-console capture are planned. This is an unsigned beta, so your OS may show a first-launch warning.

App page & downloads →

2026.11.03

Narumasouranroku page published

We published the page for Narumasouranroku, our MEGA DRIVE* game in development.

The prototype (M1) runs on an emulator, and the opening, title, conversations, battles and saving all work. Real-hardware testing comes next.

2026.11.02

Mark 16 main board (v2) ordered

We ordered the second version (v2) of the Mark 16 main board. It is a 4-layer board.

When the boards arrive, we will hand-solder the through-hole parts and assemble them.

2026.11.01

Menu running on the prototype

The Mark 16 menu is now running on the prototype hardware. It has a retro red, black and blue look and can switch between Japanese and English.
