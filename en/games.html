The Building

ザ・ビルディング　／　Mega Drive software (playable prototype)

Download

You play an office worker climbing from the 2nd floor to the rooftop of a high-rise hit by disaster (earthquake and fire), then escaping by helicopter. The intro takes you from the 1st-floor lobby into an elevator; just after you reach the 2nd floor, a large explosion hits the 1st floor (you can skip the intro with START).

Controls

D-pad left/rightWalk (you speed up, then slide a little when you let go)
A / B / CJump (hold longer to jump higher; while on a ladder, jump off)
D-pad up/downClimb or descend ladders and ropes. Hold up in mid-air to grab a ledge
D-pad downBrace yourself on a sloping floor so you do not slide
While hangingUp or forward to pull up (down or a button to let go)
A/B/C on a ropeLet go (hold left or right to leap that way)
STARTFrom the title, start the intro. Press again during the intro to skip to gameplay on the 2nd floor

What's in the game

  • Seven stages (four floors each, floors 2–28) plus the rooftop. Look for a ladder up.
  • You have 3 lives. After a miss, you restart that stage from the beginning.
  • Hazards include a rising fire, flames, falls, gas explosions, wind, sloping floors, and falling ceiling fragments.
  • Art, music and sound effects are all original.
  • Score, a timer, saving and pause are not implemented yet.

Tested on MARK 16 real hardware and on BlastEm 0.6.3.

How to play on MARK 16

  1. Create a MARK16 folder at the root of the microSD card (this is not the older MDTANG folder).
  2. Unzip the download and copy the_building.bin into MARK16/ROMS.
  3. Insert the card into the console and choose the game from the list. You can also add it with the Mark16Manager PC app.

Simple Drum

シンプルドラム　／　Mega Drive software

Download

A simple drum kit in the middle of the screen, played with the buttons on your pad. You can play freely, drum along with three original tracks in Session, or change the button mapping in Option.

Controls

The title menu is Free / Session / Option. Choose with up/down, and confirm with A, C or START. Press START during play to return to the title. MODE is not used.

ABass drum (kick)
BSnare
CFloor tom
XHigh tom
YMid tom
ZRim / clap
UpCrash cymbal
DownOpen hi-hat
LeftClosed hi-hat
RightRide cymbal

The table above is the default mapping. In Option you can change the pad type (AUTO / 6-button / 3-button), layered sound (off / on; off by default), and the sound assigned to each input. The menus can be used with a 3-button pad. Settings reset when you power off.

Session

  • 1. Rock 8-beat (default 130 BPM)
  • 2. Hard shuffle (default 156 BPM)
  • 3. Slow blues (default 84 BPM)

Adjust the tempo with left/right and start. After a 4-beat count-in, a lead guitar, bass and power chords play, and you can drum along freely. A guitarist appears on the right, and a beat counter plus a groove meter are shown at the top left (no fail condition). All drum samples are original synthesized sounds.

How to play on MARK 16

  1. Create a MARK16 folder at the root of the microSD card (this is not the older MDTANG folder).
  2. Unzip the download and copy simple_drum.bin into MARK16/ROMS.
  3. Insert the card into the console and choose the game from the list. You can also add it with the Mark16Manager PC app.
