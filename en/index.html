About

INRESET specializes in the repair and development of electronic components. With precise repair skills and our own development capabilities, we restore your equipment to its best condition and create new value.


From restoring broken circuit boards to designing and developing new circuits, we cover a wide range of needs. With fast turnaround and high quality as our motto, we support people who make things.

Services

Electronics repair

Board repair, BGA/CSP replacement and jumper wiring. We can work on discontinued products and boards without schematics. A high restoration rate helps cut costs and reduce environmental impact.

Development

Electronic circuit design and development, prototyping and mass-production support. We turn your ideas into practical products.

Custom work

Flexible support from small lots to large scale. One-stop service from diagnosis to repair and improvement.
