Mark 16

Play your own cartridges on today's TVs.
An FPGA console for MEGA DRIVE* cartridges

Not software emulation: the original circuits are reproduced in an FPGA. Insert a cartridge from the top and enjoy a clean picture on your TV through DV output.

Crowdfunding planned

Features

Your real cartridges

Top-loading design for MEGA DRIVE* cartridges. Just insert your cartridge from the top and play. Overseas GENESIS* cartridges fit as they are.

DV output for picture and sound

One cable carries both picture and sound to your TV or monitor, for a sharp image on modern screens.

Save states

Save and resume your game at any point. Save data and screenshots are stored on a microSD card.

USB controllers

Two USB controller ports for two-player games.

Improves with updates

Firmware can be updated from a microSD card. Support for 8-bit cartridges is also planned.

A nostalgic design

A red, black and blue design that recalls the 16-bit era. The menu is available in Japanese and English.

Menu screens (in development)

A retro red, black and blue menu. Switch between Japanese and English.

* Screens are from development and may change in the final product.

FAQ

Can I use overseas (GENESIS*) cartridges?

Yes. Overseas cartridges fit as they are. * We do not guarantee that all software will work.

How does it connect to a TV?

Through DV output. One cable carries both picture and sound to your TV or monitor.

Is it an emulator?

No. It is not software emulation: the original circuits are reproduced in an FPGA.

How much input latency is there?

We have a dedicated measurement feature for latency. We will publish results measured on the prototype on this site later.

When is the release, and what is the price?

We plan to sell it through crowdfunding. The start date and price are not decided yet. We will announce them as soon as they are.

Is a controller included?

Mark 16 supports USB controllers (2 USB ports). Whether to include a controller is under consideration.

Does it support save states?

Yes, planned. You will be able to save your game at any point and resume later.
