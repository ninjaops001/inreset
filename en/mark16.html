Play your own cartridges on today's TVs.
An FPGA console for MEGA DRIVE* cartridges

Not software emulation: the original circuits are reproduced in an FPGA. Insert a cartridge from the top and enjoy a clean picture on your TV through DV output.

Crowdfunding planned

Features

Your real cartridges

Top-loading design for MEGA DRIVE* cartridges. Just insert your cartridge from the top and play. Overseas GENESIS* cartridges fit as they are.

DV output for picture and sound

One cable carries both picture and sound to your TV or monitor, for a sharp image on modern screens.

Save states

On development units, you can store your progress in slots and resume later. Save data and screenshots are stored on a microSD card. Some software still has problems.

USB controllers

Two USB controller ports for two-player games. Plug in a wired Xbox controller (XInput) to use it as an analog controller. On supported games such as After Burner II, the stick works like an XE-1AP analog pad; in regular games, it also works as the D-pad. The triggers are analog too: in supported games, RT (and right stick up) raises the throttle, and LT lowers it. Xbox Start (≡) is START, and the center Xbox button opens the console menu.

Improved through updates

Firmware can be updated from a microSD card, via Settings → Console update. Support for Master System* and other 8-bit systems is also planned.

Install software you own

Play software you own from a microSD card (root folder /MARK16) or from the console’s built-in flash. The menu’s “Save cartridge” item can store an inserted cartridge to the card or to built-in flash. The Mark16Manager PC app (v0.2.3, Windows / Apple silicon and Intel Mac) helps you organise the card.

* Using illegally downloaded data and distributing game data are prohibited by law. Please use only software you legitimately own.

Mark16Manager PC app

A companion app for organising the console’s microSD card on your computer. Add games, edit thumbnails, intros, maker and release date, back up saves and save states, export selected screenshots at 4×, and delete unused firmware, screenshots and save data. A v0.2.3 beta for Windows and Mac (Apple silicon and Intel) is available for Mark 16 development units.

FAQ

For compatible consoles, FPGAs, patents, trademarks, and copyright, see the Q&A page.

Is a compatible console illegal?

Making hardware that works the same way is not, by itself, generally illegal. See Q&A: “Is a compatible console illegal?” for a careful explanation.

Which software works?

Most MEGA DRIVE* / GENESIS* software runs, but we do not guarantee that every title will work. Titles checked on hardware are listed on the compatibility list.

Can I use overseas (GENESIS*) cartridges?

Yes. Overseas cartridges fit as they are. * We do not guarantee that all software will work.

How does it connect to a TV?

Through DV output. One cable carries both picture and sound to your TV or monitor.

Is it an emulator?

No. It is not software emulation: the original circuits are reproduced in an FPGA.

How much input latency is there?

We have a dedicated measurement feature for latency. We will publish results measured on the prototype on this site later.

When is the release, and what is the price?

Crowdfunding for the console is planned for the first half of 2027. The price has not been decided yet. We will announce further details as soon as they are.

Is a controller included?

Mark 16 supports USB controllers (2 USB ports). Whether to include a controller is under consideration.

Can I use an Xbox controller?

Yes. Plug in a wired Xbox controller (XInput) to use it as an analog controller. On supported games such as After Burner II, the stick works like an XE-1AP analog pad; in regular games, it also works as the D-pad. The triggers are analog too: in supported games, RT (and right stick up) raises the throttle, and LT lowers it. Xbox Start (≡) is START, and the center Xbox button opens the console menu.

Does it support save states?

Yes, on development units. You can store your progress in slots and resume later. Some software still has problems. Saves and save states can be backed up to a computer with the PC app.
