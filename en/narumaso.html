Narumasouranroku

鳴魔奏乱録

A dramatic simulation game

Software for the MEGA DRIVE*. A story of sound-powered armor and the girls who pilot it, told through conversation scenes and quarter-view tactical battles.

Story

Ritsu Kanezaki, a former lieutenant in the army signal corps, was about to leave the military after his unit was wiped out by magical beasts at the border. Then an order arrives: a transfer to the Minatobe Broadcasting Station. He is made leader of the 7th Special Broadcasting Unit. But the unit's true nature is a squad of five girls who pilot "Meireiki", humanoid armor powered by sound. Ritsu and his squad stand against the beasts attacking the harbor, and against "Nuemeitou", a secret organization of beast tamers working behind them.

Features

Timed choices

Choices in conversations have a time limit. Your answers change your relationships with squad members and affect battles.

Meireiki

Steampunk, samurai-armor-style mecha about 3 m tall. They glide like skaters on wheels in their feet.

Tactics from the command vehicle

Quarter-view simulation battles without grid lines. The hero stays off the front line and supports the squad by relaying communications from the command vehicle.

Opening with vocals

An anime-style opening with a vocal theme song. In a cinemascope frame, multi-layer scrolling moves in time with the music.

Five unique squad members

Each has a uniform in her own color and her own Meireiki. The weapons are inspired by old Imperial Japanese Army equipment.

FAQ

Can I play it on real MEGA DRIVE* hardware?

We plan to make it playable on real hardware using a flash cartridge. Right now the prototype runs on an emulator, and real-hardware testing has not been done yet.

How far along is development?

In the prototype (M1), the opening, title, conversations, battles and saving all work. We are now polishing the opening and the battle map graphics.

When is the release, and what is the price?

Not decided yet. We will announce them as soon as they are.
